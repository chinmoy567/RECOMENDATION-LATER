# Letter review rubric (score 1-5 each)

1. **Specificity**: concrete examples with numbers/outcomes, not adjectives.
2. **Referee credibility**: relationship, duration, and authority to judge are stated.
3. **Comparative ranking**: percentile/rank vs peers and cohort size.
4. **Program relevance**: names the program; evidence maps to committee criteria (research potential for thesis, applied/leadership for course-based).
5. **Bangladesh context**: scale, rank, strictness, institution explained.
6. **Balance and honesty**: believable, one growth point, no inflation.
7. **Structure and length**: strengths early, 1-2 pages, clear closing recommendation.
8. **Language**: clear, professional, consistent spelling, no cliches.
9. **Compliance**: dated, signed, letterhead, institutional email, uploaded by the referee via portal.

## Red flags (fail on any)
- Looks written by the applicant, or template text reused across applicants.
- Unverified or fabricated facts, inflated titles.
- Generic phrases with no evidence.
- Wrong program name, dates, or pronouns.
- Irrelevant personal information.

## Output
Scorecard table, top 5 fixes ranked by impact, rewrites of the 3 weakest sentences, and a pass / needs-revision verdict.
