# Bangladesh context for Canadian committees

Committees may not know the Bangladeshi scale. The referee is the best-placed person to explain it.

## Include (facts only, referee-verified)
- Scale: CGPA out of 4.00 (or 5.00) and the university's grading policy. Canadian universities do their own conversion (e.g. SFU requires roughly a B- equivalent overall; WLU and others publish country tables). Never invent a conversion.
- Rank and cohort: "3rd of 120 in the 2021-25 batch", "top 5%".
- Strictness: typical class average; how rare a first class / A+ is in the department.
- Institution: public/private, accreditation (UGC, BAETE where relevant), standing; 4-year bachelor's meets Canadian equivalence, and some schools require credential evaluation (e.g. WES).
- Medium of instruction: English; thesis/project written in English (English test requirements still apply separately).
- Constraints turned into achievement: limited labs/compute, self-taught skills, competitions, open-source work.
- A CGPA dip: one factual sentence, referee's judgment.

## Avoid
- Claiming a Canadian-GPA or percentage equivalent without an official conversion.
- Name-dropping unrelated institutions.
- Stereotyped praise; keep it merit-based.

## Note to applicant
A strong letter supports a profile; it does not replace required grades, English test, and statement of purpose. Check each program's minimums.
